# The 2 AM Choice That's Destroying Your Peace
## Shreya vs. Preya • Insight #1

From the Katha Upanishad

---

You made this choice today.

At 2 AM, scrolling instead of sleeping. Ordering takeout instead of having the difficult conversation. Numbing out instead of feeling.

This wasn't random. It was a choice between two paths.

**Shreya vs. Preya.** *Shreya* (श्रेय) — the good. *Preya* (प्रेय) — the pleasant. What builds you vs. what numbs you.

Every time you choose the pleasant, your anxiety compounds. Every time you choose the good, it decreases.

You've been choosing wrong your whole life. Not because you're weak — because nobody taught you to see the choice.
