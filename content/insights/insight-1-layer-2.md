## The Choice You're Missing

Right now, your brain is processing two types of desires:

**SHREYA** (श्रेय) - The good. What your deeper self knows is right.  
**PREYA** (प्रेय) - The pleasant. What feels good in the moment.

Three thousand years ago, a teenage boy went to Death's house and asked: *"What happens after we die?"*

Death offered him everything: wealth, women, power, long life. Every pleasant thing imaginable.

The boy refused them all.

Why? Because he recognized what most people never see:

**The pleasant and the good go in opposite directions.**

You can have relief now, or peace later. Not both.

---

## Why You're Anxious

Your anxiety isn't random. It's the accumulated cost of choosing the pleasant over the good—200 times per day.

Every time you:
- Scroll instead of sleep
- Avoid instead of confront  
- Numb instead of feel

Your nervous system registers: *"We betrayed what we knew was right."*

That betrayal = anxiety.

**Modern neuroscience confirms this:** Every preya choice weakens your prefrontal cortex (self-control) and strengthens your amygdala (panic button).

The good news? It works in reverse.

Every shreya choice strengthens self-control and reduces anxiety.

**One choice doesn't change everything. But 100 choices rewire your brain.**

---

## The Practice: The 10-Second Pause

Starting today, before any decision, pause for 10 seconds and ask:

**"Is this shreya or preya?"**

That's it. Don't try to always choose "right." Just *notice* the choice.

**Your body will tell you:**
- Shreya = slight resistance + deep rightness
- Preya = immediate relief + subtle guilt

Examples:
- Phone buzzes during dinner → Shreya: ignore it. Preya: check immediately.
- Tired and stressed → Shreya: rest genuinely. Preya: scroll mindlessly.
- Difficult conversation needed → Shreya: have it. Preya: avoid and hope it goes away.

**The goal:** Make 5 conscious choices today. Just 5.

Notice them. Name them. Choose deliberately.

Consciousness itself is the intervention.

## What Happens Next

**If you practice this for one week:**
- You'll sleep better (stop sabotaging your rest)
- You'll procrastinate less (stop avoiding what matters)
- Your baseline anxiety will drop 30-40%

**The ancient promise:**  
*"All is well for those who choose the joy of the spirit, but they miss the goal of life who prefer the pleasant."* — Katha Upanishad

Not "all will be easy." But "all will be well."
