# Meet the Part of You That Never Panics
## The Witness Self • Sakshi

From the Katha Upanishad

---

It's 2 AM. Your mind won't stop.

*"I'm not good enough. I'll never succeed. Everyone sees through me."*

And you believe every word—because you think YOU are these thoughts.

But here's the question nobody asks: **If you ARE the thought… who's the one watching it?**

---

### What You'll Learn:

- **The observer within** - Discover Sakshi (साक्षी), the Witness: the part of you that can watch your anxious thoughts without drowning in them

- **The sky vs. the clouds** - Your thoughts are like clouds passing through the sky. They come and go. But you—the sky—remain unchanged

- **How to stop fighting your mind** - You've been battling your thoughts your whole life. Learn the ancient practice of simply witnessing them instead
